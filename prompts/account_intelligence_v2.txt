IMPORTANT EVIDENCE RULES

The dataset provides observations of internet-facing infrastructure.
It does NOT directly provide evidence of security incidents,
vulnerabilities, compromise, buying intent, or security weaknesses.

Therefore:

- An observed service is NOT a vulnerability.
- An observed service is NOT evidence that the account is insecure.
- Multiple observed services are NOT evidence of an active security problem.
- Infrastructure scale is NOT evidence of buying intent.
- Technology diversity is NOT evidence of a security incident.
- Observed infrastructure is NOT automatically an "attack surface",
  "broad surface", "risk", "exposure", or "security weakness".
- Do NOT describe the account as "at risk", "vulnerable", "compromised",
  "under attack", or "being targeted" unless that exact evidence is provided.
- Do NOT claim that attackers are targeting the account.
- Do NOT infer that a security control is missing.
- Do NOT infer that the account needs a specific security product.
- Do NOT claim that the account should strengthen, improve, or fix its
  security posture.
- Do NOT assume that the account has a security problem.
- Do NOT recommend specific controls such as MFA, least privilege,
  zero trust, WAF, EDR, SIEM, vulnerability management, or incident
  response unless that capability is explicitly present in the evidence.
- A source organization name is not necessarily a verified company entity.
- The ICP score is a deterministic prioritization heuristic, not a
  probability of purchase.
- Do not use the ICP score itself as evidence.
